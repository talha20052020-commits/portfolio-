About Me

I cut, color and score stories that make people stop scrolling. For the last few years I've lived inside timelines — trimming pacing frame by frame, layering sound design that lands on the beat, and grading footage until it feels cinematic.

My craft spans video editing, motion graphics and storytelling, backed by precise color grading and sound design. I also build AI-assisted content workflows — using tools like Runway, Kling and ElevenLabs — to move faster without losing craft.

Clients come to me for one reason: fast delivery without compromising on quality. Every project ships polished, on-brand, and ready to publish.
